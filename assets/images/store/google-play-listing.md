## Full description (4000 chars max)

Nourish is your complete anti-inflammatory meal plan system — built for people living with chronic pain, autoimmune conditions, arthritis, and inflammation-related fatigue who want a clear, guided path to eating for healing.

**What's inside Nourish:**

🥗 **Complete Meal Plans**
Structured weekly meal plans designed around anti-inflammatory whole foods. Each plan is built to reduce the foods that trigger inflammation and fill your body with the nutrients it needs to recover.

📖 **Step-by-Step Recipes**
Every meal comes with a detailed recipe — ingredients, instructions, and the why behind each choice. No guessing, no complicated techniques.

🛒 **Smart Shopping Lists**
Auto-generated grocery lists organized by store section. Walk in, check things off, and leave with everything you need for the week.

📊 **Nutrition Tracker** *(Pro)*
Log your daily meals and track key anti-inflammatory nutrients — omega-3s, fiber, antioxidants — so you can see the pattern between what you eat and how you feel.

🔥 **Inflammation Tracker**
Log your daily energy, mood, pain, sleep, stress, and water intake. Track patterns over time to understand your personal inflammation triggers.

📈 **Health Reports**
View weekly, monthly, and long-term trends. Export your data as PDF, plain text, or Markdown to share with your doctor or health coach.

👤 **Personal Profile**
Store your wellness information privately on your device. Your data is yours.

🧘 **Wellness Toolkit**
Vetted wellness devices and tools — including our Sensate partnership — to address the stress side of inflammation that food alone can't always reach.

**Who is Nourish for?**

- People living with rheumatoid arthritis, lupus, Crohn's, or other autoimmune conditions
- Anyone dealing with chronic joint pain, fatigue, or brain fog
- People who've been told by their doctor to reduce inflammation through diet
- Anyone who wants to eat healthier but needs structure and guidance
