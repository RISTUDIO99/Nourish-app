## Section 1: Audio Library

### What it is
Pre-recorded audio tracks that members hit play on inside the app. Think scripted, calm, focused — like a wellness podcast but exclusive to your legacy members. 10–15 minutes each.

### What you need to record

| Track | Topic | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Morning Inflammation Reset | A guided morning routine — how to start the day with intention, light movement, anti-inflammatory mindset | ~12 min |
| Gut Healing Breathwork | Breathwork session specifically tied to gut inflammation — diaphragmatic breathing, pacing, cues | ~8 min |
| Anti-Inflammatory Mindset | Joseph talking through the mental side of living with RA — mindset shifts that helped him heal | ~18 min |
| Evening Wind-Down Protocol | Closing the day — what to eat, what to avoid after 6pm, calming the nervous system | ~10 min |

### How to record it
- **Phone voice memo app is fine to start.** Quality matters but perfection doesn't.
- Find a quiet room. Speak slowly and clearly.
- Script it out first in bullet points, then talk through it naturally — don't read word-for-word.
- Aim for WAV or M4A format. I will convert to MP3 for the app.

### How it gets into the app
1. Record on your phone → AirDrop or email to yourself
2. Send the files to me
3. I build the audio player screen and embed them — members tap play, it streams

## Section 2: Legacy Video Series

### What it is
Pre-recorded video sessions — NOT live Zoom calls. Think YouTube-style but exclusive, unlisted, only available inside the app. Members watch these on their own time. Evergreen content.

*(The 1-on-1 Zoom calls are a separate monthly perk — live, personal, scheduled. These videos are different.)*

### What you need to record

| Video | Topic | Length |
|---|---|---|
| The Anti-Inflammatory Blueprint | Your full framework — what to eat, what to cut, the science in plain language | ~20 min |
| Advanced Meal Prep: 60 Min, 7 Days | Walk through exactly how you prep a full week of anti-inflammatory meals in one hour | ~20 min |
| RA & Nutrition: What the Research Says | Break down the research — what actually works, what's overblown, what helped you | ~15 min |
| Building Routines That Actually Stick | How to turn all of this into a habit — the psychology, the practical system | ~15 min |

### How to record it
- **iPhone camera is more than enough.** Tripod or propped against books.
- Good lighting matters more than the camera — face a window.
- Lapel mic ($20–$30 on Amazon) makes a huge difference in audio quality.
- Keep it conversational. You know this material cold — don't over-script it.

### How it gets into the app
1. Record → upload to **YouTube as Unlisted** (or Vimeo if you prefer privacy)
2. Send me the video link
3. I embed it directly inside the vault screen — it plays inside the app, members never leave

## Section 3: Weekly Premium Content

### What it is
Short articles that live inside the app — updated regularly. Think of it as your private newsletter, but members read it in the vault instead of their email inbox. One new piece per week is a solid cadence.

### What you need to write (recurring, weekly)

Each week, aim for **one of the following** (rotate through):
- **Seasonal ingredient spotlight** — "This week: turmeric. Here's how to use it, why it works, and a simple recipe."
- **Research summary** — one recent study, explained in plain English, with your take
- **Exclusive recipe** — a new anti-inflammatory recipe not in the main meal plans
- **Protocol update** — "What I've been doing differently this month and why"

### Format
- Title
- 2–4 paragraphs of content
- Optional: one image or a simple recipe card
- No need to be long — 300–500 words per post is plenty

### How it gets into the app
Two options — we can decide together:
1. **You send me a Google Doc each week** → I add it to the app manually (simple but requires me each time)
2. **We build a simple content management system** → you log in to a dashboard, paste your content, hit publish, and it appears in the app automatically (more setup now, fully hands-off forever after)

### Your to-do
- [ ] Write your first weekly content piece — pick any format above to start
- [ ] Decide: manual delivery vs. self-serve dashboard
